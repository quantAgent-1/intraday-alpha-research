## DR-X7 — Practitioner/Vendor (modality C) findings

### Verdict recommendation
**EXHAUSTED-BY-FIELD (confidence MED)** — for the specific M19 premise (single-stock LETF re-hedging creates a *distributed-intraday* footprint that *mean-reverts intraday*). The practitioner/vendor field is unanimous and consistent: the LETF hedge flow is **close-concentrated** (final 30–60 min, benchmarked to the 16:00 NAV), it is **negative-gamma / momentum** intraday (amplifying, not reverting), and the reversal it produces is **overnight** (out-of-mission) — not an intraday fade. The only coherent single-stock LETF edge practitioners describe is the close-window push, which the champion *already* monetizes (M12 Cell A; the +12.5 bps holdout basis trade at the cross). The intraday front-run of the same flow is textbook (Chan 2013), HFT-arbitraged, and net-of-cost ≈ 0. No named practitioner or vendor advocates or sells an intraday-reverting single-stock LETF metric.
**What would flip it:** a named practitioner/vendor documenting that swap counterparties *pre-hedge single-stock LETF exposure on a distributed intraday schedule* AND that this pre-hedge systematically *overshoots and reverts within RTH before the close* (distinct from the last-30-min option-gamma reversal and distinct from the overnight reversal). I found zero sources describing this; every source describes drift-then-fix-at-close.

### Mechanism
**Who pays / why price-insensitive:** A single-stock LETF (NVDL 2×NVDA, TSLL 2×TSLA, Direxion 1.5×/2× NVDA, MU/MARA products) must reset exposure to a constant multiple of its *closing* NAV every day. Because required exposure scales super-linearly, its mechanical demand is **gamma ∝ (L²−L)·ΔS** — Abdelmessih's `X(X−1)(ΔS)`, identical to the champion's F=(L²−L)·AUM·r (M12 Cell A). The fund/swap counterparty is **price-insensitive**: it must buy after the underlying rises and sell after it falls to hit the daily-reset mandate, regardless of price. This is **negative gamma** — destabilizing, trend-amplifying.
**When it pays:** The contractual benchmark is the **close**. Practitioners uniformly place the *executed* flow in the **final 30–60 minutes / at the closing auction**, benchmarked to the 16:00 print. Abdelmessih is explicit that the swap dealer's delta is allowed to **drift through the day and is fixed at the close** — i.e., dealers do *not* run a continuous intraday hedge that would leave a distributed footprint. Ernie Chan / Rodier et al. state LETFs "unequivocally implement rebalancing at the close."
**Why it persists / capacity:** The daily-reset mandate is structural and the AUM is now large vs the underlyings' *closing-window* liquidity (see C5) — so the close-window imbalance is real and persistent. But it pays **into the close**, and its documented mean-reversion is **overnight** (the "buy-high/sell-low own-footprint" cost, ≈1.3%/yr), which is out-of-mission. There is **no coherent payer for an intraday-reverting single-name footprint**: intraday, the same flow is momentum (negative gamma), and any last-30-min *reversal* that exists is attributed to **option-dealer gamma**, a different payer, not LETF re-hedging.

### Economics sketch
- **Front-run of the close flow (momentum, index LETFs):** Rodier et al. gross **0.60%/trade**, breakeven cost **0.60%** → **net ≈ 0/trade** (2006–2011, NO-COST-MODEL beyond the stated breakeven; profits concentrated in the 2008 vol spike; decay flagged). Does not clear our bar and is not reverting.
- **Intraday single-stock reversion (the M19 thesis):** no measured effect exists to size. Per-event noise here ≈ 20 bps sd; you would need n≥250 to detect a mean you have no prior for — and no practitioner/vendor supplies that prior.
- **Where the LETF value actually books:** the close-window single-name push is captured *at the cross* by the champion's NOII basis trade (single print, no fill ambiguity). Champion = **+2.5 bps/event dev / +12.5 bps holdout**; M12 Cell A already attributes its name-selectivity to (L²−L)·AUM·r. The LETF mechanism's monetizable value is therefore **already booked at the close** — it is not sitting unclaimed intraday.

### Charge deliverables (required close-out)
**(a) Practitioner-consensus timing split:** Not a smooth split — the flow is **essentially all in the final 30–60 minutes, benchmarked to the 16:00 NAV** (Abdelmessih, Innes, Chan/Rodier, Nomura, Tier1Alpha/Kobeissi all agree; dealers let delta **drift** and fix at close). Discretionary pre-hedging can begin ~14:00–15:30 but is **momentum-aligned** (front-runners *ride* it into the close), not a distributed reverting footprint. A morning/midday distributed single-stock LETF hedge component is **UNKNOWN/undocumented** — treated as unhedged drift, not executed flow. Practitioner estimate: **~0% distributed-and-reverting intraday / ~100% close-window (with a momentum pre-hedge tail into the last hour).**
**(b) Intraday pressure metrics practitioners actually compute:** (i) **LETF rebalance imbalance** ΩLETF ∝ (L²−L)·AUM·r_intraday, sized vs last-30-min ADV — desk forms: Nomura "$9bn per 1% move," Tier1Alpha/Kobeissi "$50bn/day"; and (ii) **dealer GEX/gamma** (SpotGamma, MenthorQ). Inputs are PIT near the close (public AUM, known leverage, live intraday return). All **predict the close flow**; none is an intraday-reversion metric, and no vendor sells one for single names.
**(c) Does M19 deserve registration? (honest paragraph):** On practitioner/vendor evidence, **no — not in the intraday-distributed-reverting framing.** Every named source (Abdelmessih, Innes, Chan, Nomura, Kobeissi/Tier1Alpha) converges on close-concentrated, negative-gamma *momentum* flow whose only reversal is *overnight* (out-of-mission) or a *last-30-min option-gamma* reversal (a different payer). The single coherent single-stock LETF edge is the close-window push, and the champion already owns it (M12 Cell A, +12.5 bps holdout) — registering M19 as an intraday variant would re-test an owned mechanism on the wrong clock, against continuous-market fills, with no measured reverting effect to power against. The house doctrine bar ("register only on triggers with demonstrated conditional mid-alpha") is **not met**: there is no practitioner-demonstrated intraday-reverting mid-alpha here, only motivation. If synthesis still wants to spend a trial, the *only* practitioner-supported reframing worth a base-rate study is **overnight** reversal of the close push on high-AUM single names — but that is explicitly out-of-mission (would need user amendment for overnight holds) and should be flagged as such, not registered as an RTH intraday trade.
