## DR-X7 — Modality D (adversarial / prior-art) findings

### Verdict recommendation
**NOT-VIABLE-STRUCTURAL (confidence MED-HIGH)** — with a strong secondary **EXHAUSTED-BY-FIELD** flavor. The mandated single-stock-LETF re-hedge flow is end-of-day / market-on-close, not intraday, so an *intraday* re-hedge payer is ≈0. The one academic paper that actually documents a single-name end-of-day price *reversal* (Baltussen-Da-Soebhag 2025) places it in the **last 30 minutes** (the champion's window, ρ≈1) and explicitly finds it is **not** driven by liquidity/gamma/LETF hedging — the drivers are retail attention and short-covering. Where LETF flow *does* act (market level) it drives last-half-hour **momentum** that "reverts over the next days" (multi-day, out-of-mission), not intraday reversion. So conditioning on an LETF flow-state F has no coherent intraday-reversion payer and, to the extent it works at all, re-discovers a close-window return signal the champion already monetizes.

**What would flip it:** Project-internal 1-second evidence that NVDA/MU specifically exhibit an *intraday* (pre-15:50) price dislocation that reliably reverts **within the session** and is predictable by F with realized-PnL correlation to the close book well below ρ (0.83–0.98). External prior-art cannot close that specific door because every causal LETF-impact paper predates the single-stock-LETF era (2022+) and studies EOD/index flow only — but external prior-art also gives it zero affirmative support.

### Mechanism
No coherent *intraday* payer found. The mechanical, price-insensitive flow that M19 needs is the LETF leverage reset, and that flow is definitionally end-of-day: to deliver a constant *daily* multiple the fund/swap counterparty must be at target exposure at the NAV strike (the close), and holding the hedge intraday only adds overnight/margin/borrow risk it has "little choice but to" avoid by hedging at the end of the day (Baltussen-Da 2021, §motivation). Shum et al. (2015) measure LETF rebalancing as **16.8% / 50.2% of market-on-close volume** on days the market moves 1% / 5% — i.e., the flow shows up in the *closing auction*, exactly where the champion already trades. The residual intraday component is (a) the swap dealer's discretionary delta management, which the option-MM literature shows *also* clusters in the last half hour (hedging price changes accumulated to ~15:30), and (b) small "intraday rebalancing slippage" / tail-event risk rebalances that are not routine, directional, or predictable. Net: intraday re-hedge share ≈ 0 for tradable purposes; the payer fires at the close.

Separately, the recent single-stock-ETF impact literature attributes the underlying-stock effect to a **persistent idiosyncratic-volatility increase** driven by retail attention + short-selling (Huang 2025) and to a **price-discovery/informational** component (Jain-Mishra-Pagano-Rodriguez). A price-discovery component is *permanent*, not mean-reverting — fading it loses money. This is the opposite of the pure-liquidity-pressure-that-snaps-back that a reversion trade requires.

### Claims
C1 [CONFIRMED] (T2, JFE 2021, sample 1993–2018 US + intl; via Shum et al. 2015): LETF rebalancing is a **market-on-close** phenomenon — 16.8% (50.2%) of MOC volume on 1% (5%) move days; "such as LETFs have little choice but to hedge at the end of the day" because overnight/margin/borrow costs make intraday hedging suboptimal. The intraday share of the mandated rebalance is ≈0. — Baltussen, Da et al., "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum," ScienceDirect S0304405X21001598.
C2 [CONFIRMED] (T2, April 2025, sample not-yet-pinned/high-freq US): The single-name **end-of-day reversal** — the exact object M19 would trade — is confined to the **last 30 minutes** and **"cannot be explained by liquidity- or gamma-hedging effects"**; drivers are attention-induced retail purchases + short-seller risk management. A bottom-minus-top ROD3 decile long-short earns **0.24%/day** in the final half-hour (GROSS, NO-COST-MODEL, cross-sectional decile). Double-kill: the reversal lives in the champion's window (ρ≈1) *and* has a non-LETF payer. — Baltussen, Da, Soebhag, "End-of-Day Reversal," SSRN 5039009.
C3 [CONFIRMED] (T2, JFE 2021): Where LETF/option gamma-hedging flow *does* move price, it produces last-half-hour **momentum (continuation)** predicted by the rest-of-day return, and that pressure **"reverts over the next days"** — multi-day, overnight-spanning (out-of-mission), not an intraday snap-back. — same as C1.
C4 [CONFIRMED] (T2, 2018 sample 2006–2014 + 2024 survey): After accounting for offsetting capital flows and risk factors, the LETF rebalance impact on late-day returns/volatility is **economically insignificant**; the 2024 survey adds that most such studies "suffer from potentially serious methodological errors" and the associations "nonetheless appear to be insignificant." Little/no dislocation to revert. — Ivanov & Lenkey, J. Fin. Markets 41 (2018); Lenkey survey, AIMS QFE 2024 / SSRN 4710812.
C5 [CONFIRMED] (T2/T3, sample Jun-2006→Jul-2011): The tradable "front-run the EOD LETF flow" strategy is **continuation**, not reversion; gross **0.60%/trade** but **profit concentrated in the 2008 stress window**, GROSS not net, and "market adaptation may have reduced profitability." Independent replication (Ernie-Chan-style LETF intraday momentum) "flattens out beginning 2017" and is "unprofitable after transaction costs." Crowded + decayed. — Rodier-Haryanto-Shum-Hejazi (2012) via CXO Advisory; QuantRocket "Intraday Momentum with Leveraged ETFs."
C6 [CONFIRMED] (T3, 2022/2026): The EOD rebalance is public and predictable — now "a primary driver of late-day price action" at a record **$50B/day, 1.60% of ES volume** (Jul 2026) — and predictable flows are front-run by hedge funds/prop (Sida Li; leveraged-VIX-flow evidence). Anticipation is a milliseconds game at/into the auction; nothing is left for a 5–25s manual reaction in the continuous market. — Benzinga (Jul 2026); Li (2022) via Armchair Trader.
C7 [PLAUSIBLE] (T2, Oct-2025 / EFMA-2024): The single-stock-ETF effect on the underlying is a **persistent idiosyncratic-volatility increase** (retail attention + short-selling), and LETF flow has an **informational/price-discovery component** — both non-mean-reverting. Higher vol ≠ tradable reversion; a price-discovery push is permanent. — Huang, SSRN 5691524; Jain-Mishra-Pagano-Rodriguez, "Of Seesaws and Swings."
C8 [CONFIRMED] (T2, Jul-2025): Levered single-stock-ETF rebalancing is a **deterministic drag on the ETF holder** (−0.26%/mo rebalancing + −0.53%/mo frictions, total −0.79%/mo underperformance), *not* a revertible dislocation in the underlying stock. The widely-cited "NVDL −67% while NVDA −35%" is compounding/volatility decay inside the ETF — conflating it with a tradable NVDA reversion is a category error (and a known trap). — Bessembinder, SSRN 5369417.
C9 [PLAUSIBLE→adversarial] (internal, ledger 2026-07-20): The M18 ember (NVDA mid-alpha +$2.19/trade, MU +$2.15) is ≈1–2σ pre-cost on the **best 2 of 4 names** inside a **12-cell grid** (hold axis empirically inert → 4 effective cells) drawn from a **~160-trial** inherited sub-hour family. Expected spurious ≥2σ one-sided hits from ~160 trials ≈ 3–4 — so two marginal positives sit **at/below the noise floor**; net is **−$0.94** (fee-killed), pooled system **−$3.46/trade, n=1,494**. Textbook multiple-testing residue. — research/experiments/M18-reversion-system/report.md.

### Economics sketch
Expected gross: the only *reversion* magnitude in the literature is the cross-sectional last-30-min decile long-short of **0.24%/day gross, no-cost, non-LETF** (Baltussen-Da-Soebhag) — not attributable to F and living in the close window. The project's own intraday-reversion read is the M18 ember: **+$2.19/trade mid-alpha ≈ ~2 bps at ~$10k notional, gross +$1.79, net −$0.94** after $0.005/sh/side. Cost burden for a continuous-market intraday structure is exactly what kills it (fee + 0.5 bps taker slip + touch-fill adverse selection). Net prior: **negative** at any accessible retail tier under the registered cost model. Comparison line: **champion = +2.5 bps/event dev / +12.5 bps/event holdout** (single-print auction, mechanism-backed, 5/5 survivor) — M19-intraday scores 1/5 with a negative net prior. There is no version of these numbers where an intraday reversion clears S_new > ρ·S_book against a book with ρ 0.83–0.98 and S_book 0.249/day.

### Proposed next test
Not OPEN-TESTABLE on external evidence — recommend **kill-before-build for the intraday-reversion framing**. The only residual worth the project's *own* data (not a new external search): a purely descriptive, pre-registered base-rate study asking whether NVDA/MU show a pre-15:50 dislocation that reverts *before* 15:50 and whose sign is predicted by F, with realized-PnL ρ to the close book reported up front. If that descriptive study comes back null (as C1–C3 predict), the reversion question closes permanently; if it shows an in-session, low-ρ, F-predicted reversal, *that* — not anything in the prior-art — is the ex-ante mechanism prior M19 would need. It would charge the intraday reversion family (M18 lineage, ~160 inherited sub-hour trials — so the a-priori bar must be set for that multiplicity, not a fresh 5% level).
