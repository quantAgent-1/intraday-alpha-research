## DR-X7 — Modality B (primary/venue) findings

### Verdict recommendation
**NOT-VIABLE-STRUCTURAL** for M19-as-charged (intraday price pressure that mean-reverts) — **(confidence MED-HIGH)**. Every single-stock-LETF complex on our names commits, in *primary prospectus language*, to executing the daily re-hedge **at/near the close** as a single close-referenced reset — not distributed intraday. The re-hedge is therefore a **close single-print event**, so there is no disclosed mechanism for continuous intraday pressure that would revert *intraday*; and any close→next-session reversal can only be captured by (a) trading WITH the flow into the cross (that is the existing champion basis trade) or (b) fading and holding overnight (out-of-mission). The intraday-reversion framing collapses into the already-dead M18 zone. A **narrow OPEN-TESTABLE carve-out survives**: an ex-ante-computable flow-state proxy `F_t` as a *feature/conditioner for the existing 15:55:10 close trade* — but it risks redundancy with the NOII imbalance the champion already observes (M9 ceiling).
**What would flip it:** primary evidence that swap counterparties hedge **continuously intraday** (distributed delta management that prints in the continuous market and reverts before the cross) rather than at the close-referenced reset — I found none; every document says the opposite.

### Mechanism
Single-stock LETFs (2x long NVDA/TSLA/AMD/GOOGL/MU etc.) obtain leverage almost entirely through **total-return swaps** (plus listed options and some direct stock). To hold constant daily leverage `L`, end-of-day swap notional must equal `L × NAV`, and NAV itself is the leveraged close-to-close return — so each day the fund must **buy exposure after an up day and sell after a down day**, i.e. a *same-direction, momentum-amplifying, price-insensitive* flow. This is the textbook price-insensitive payer: the fund **must** rebalance regardless of price to stay within its stated objective. Size of the rebalance is the standard identity `(L²−L)·AUM·r` (Cheng–Madhavan 2009); for L=2 that is `2·AUM·r`. The flow is *mechanical* and *predictable*, which is exactly why it is heavily front-run (documented) and why the residual, post-crowding edge is narrow (M12: +0.510 bps/sd). **Who pays:** the LETF must transact at the close to price its swap reset off the official closing print; the swap counterparty (bank dealer) hedges its book, and its hedge is anchored to that closing price. **Why it persists:** the rebalancing is a regulatory/structural obligation of a daily-reset fund, not a discretionary trade. **Capacity:** the aggregate flow is now ~$50B/day and 1.6% of S&P futures volume (Jul-2026, up ~4×YTD) — at our $10k size capacity is a non-issue; the binding constraint is crowding/adverse-selection at the cross, not our footprint.

### Claims
**C1 [CONFIRMED] (T1, GraniteShares prospectus 2025-03-17; Direxion 485APOS 2026; Leverage Shares 2026-06-10; T-Rex/EOT 497 2025-2026):** All four complexes on our names commit in prospectus text to executing the daily rebalance **at/near the close**. GraniteShares: *"the adviser will adjust the swap notional exposure daily by sending orders to the swap provider(s) for execution at close."* Leverage Shares: *"The Fund's exposure is reset daily, typically at the close of trading."* Direxion & T-Rex (identical template): *"At the close of the markets each trading day, the Adviser rebalances the Fund's portfolio so that its exposure to [underlying] is consistent with the Fund's investment objective."* — GraniteShares S&L Single-Stock ETFs prospectus; Direxion 485APOS d237945; leverageshares.com FNG summary prospectus; ETF Opportunities Trust 497 (TSLT).

**C2 [CONFIRMED] (T1, GraniteShares 2025-03-17):** The reset targets `Daily Leverage Factor × NAV at the end of each trading day`, and *"Each Fund rebalances its exposure on a daily basis, increasing exposure in response to that day's gains or reducing exposure in response to that day's losses in the underlying stock."* → the hedge desk responds to the **since-prior-close daily percentage change**, not a rolling window; direction is same-as-move (momentum/amplifying). r-window is **disclosure-pinned to since-prior-close**.

**C3 [CONFIRMED formula / PLAUSIBLE PIT] (T2, Cheng–Madhavan 2009 "Dynamics of Leveraged and Inverse ETFs"):** Rebalance notional `= (L²−L)·AUM·r` is the accepted identity (end-of-day target `L·A(1+Lr)` minus current `L·A(1+r)`). All three inputs are PIT-knowable at 15:55:10: `L` from the owned fund registry; `AUM(t) ≈ AUM_{T-1}·(1+L·r_t)` from a T-1 N-PORT/shares anchor scaled by the live leveraged return; `r_t` = live since-prior-close return. **One non-PIT wrinkle:** same-day *net creation/redemption* changes shares outstanding and is only disclosed T+1 — use T-1 shares as the PIT anchor and treat AP flow as bounded noise.

**C4 [CONFIRMED] (T1, Direxion 485APOS 2026):** The *only* disclosed intraday-execution component is **threshold-triggered, not continuous**: *"In response to significant intraday market volatility, among other actions, the Adviser may determine to trade a portion or all of the rebalance trade for the Fund prior to market close, which may result in the Fund not achieving its investment objective."* No issuer discloses a numeric intraday-vs-close split, and none discloses routine continuous intraday hedging (explicit grep for "throughout the day / continuously / intraday hedge" returned nothing in the GraniteShares text).

**C5 [UNKNOWN] (T1 gap):** The swap **counterparty's own book-hedging schedule** is disclosed nowhere in any primary document. The fund's *order* is priced "for execution at close" and the swap is *valued at the closing price* (see C7), which economically anchors the dealer's hedge to the closing print — but whether the dealer pre-hedges/warehouses continuously vs. trades the cross is **not disclosed**. This is the one genuinely open mechanics question, and it is the pivot for any *intraday*-pressure thesis.

**C6 [CONFIRMED] (T1, N-CSR/N-CSRS filings):** Named swap counterparties — **GraniteShares:** Cowen Financial Products LLC (main/initial), Clear Street LLC, Goldman Sachs (per NVDL N-CSR). **Direxion:** Citibank N.A., UBS Securities LLC, Bank of America Merrill Lynch, Barclays, BNP Paribas, Société Générale, Morgan Stanley (counts from Direxion N-CSRS schedule of investments). **Leverage Shares/Themes & T-Rex/REX:** "major (global) financial institutions" (BNP Paribas named as Leverage Shares' liquidity/swap partner). Intermediation is spread across ~8 bulge-bracket + niche (Cowen, Clear Street) dealers.

**C7 [CONFIRMED] (T1, Direxion N-CSRS; Nasdaq Closing Cross FAQ):** *"Swap contracts are valued using the closing price of the underlying reference entity"* — the reset is **close-price-referenced**. Venue: Nasdaq disseminates the Net Order Imbalance Indicator (NOII) from **3:50pm ET**, stops accepting MOC at **3:55**, LOC at **3:58**, crosses at **4:00**; ~10% of Nasdaq ADV executes in the closing cross. The close-executed LETF flow prints as MOC/market interest in this cross — **the same NOII feed the champion reads at 15:55:10.**

**C8 [CONFIRMED scale, T3] (2026):** Aggregate LETF rebalance flow ≈ **$50B/day and 1.60% of S&P 500 futures volume** as of Jul-2026, up ~4× since Jan-2026 (Benzinga/traders); Nomura ≈ **$9B per 1% market move**. Single-stock LETFs on our names are large and concentrated: **TSLL (Direxion 2x TSLA) ~$6.3B, NVDL (GraniteShares 2x NVDA) ~$4.4B**, and the **MU 2x complex ~$4.5B (Direxion MUU $3.7B + GraniteShares MULL $0.78B)** — reconciling M12's "$22M→$6B" MU ramp as the aggregate complex.

**C9 [CONFIRMED mechanism / context] (T3):** The flow is genuinely **price-insensitive** (mandatory rebalance) but **widely known and front-run** — *"Proprietary trading desks have been known to front-run these end-of-day trades."* Consistent with M12's real-but-narrow residual (+0.510 bps/sd with controls): the mechanical prior is correct, the tradable residual is thin because the flow is crowded.

**C10 [STRUCTURAL]:** Because the flow lands **at the close (single print)**, any LETF-driven reversal is a **close→next-session** phenomenon, not an intraday one. Capturing it requires either trading WITH the flow INTO the cross (= the existing champion basis trade, already +12.5 bps holdout) or **fading + holding overnight** (out-of-mission). Intraday pre-close reversion capture is precisely the gated-intraday-reversion zone M18 already falsified.

### Economics sketch
Realistic magnitude of the `F_t` signal's marginal contribution is the M12 close-window figure: **+0.510 bps/sd with controls** — and M12 already showed `F` largely *explains the champion's name-selectivity*, i.e. it is partly **already embedded** in the live basis trade. Cost burden at the close single-print is near-zero explicit (zero commission through 2026-12-31; MOC pays no spread); the true cost is adverse selection/crowding at the cross. **Net prior for a STANDALONE `F_t` intraday-reversion trade: negative-to-flat** (M18 receipt). **Net prior for `F_t` as a champion feature: small-positive but at high risk of redundancy** — the *actual* closing imbalance is observed in the NOII at 15:55:10, which M8/M9 already exploit, and M9 concluded the 15:55:10 snapshot GBDT is the ceiling (path/evolution features added nothing). `F_t` is a *model* of the imbalance the champion already *observes*. Comparison line: **champion = +2.5 bps/event dev / +12.5 holdout**; any `F_t` variant must be judged as *incremental* over that, and the honest prior is that the increment is small and possibly zero.

### Proposed next test (narrow OPEN-TESTABLE carve-out only)
Register **at most one** ex-ante test, and only as a champion-feature study (NOT a new intraday family):
- **Hypothesis:** the ex-ante mechanical imbalance `F_t = Σ_funds (L²−L)·AUM_{T-1,fund}·(1+L·r_t)·r_t` (summed over all LETFs referencing name X), computed at 15:55:10 from owned T-1 anchors + live return, adds directional/economic information **over and above the observed NOII near-vs-mid basis** for the close trade on the 5–12 concentrated names.
- **Named payer:** single-stock-LETF mandatory close rebalance (price-insensitive; per C1–C3).
- **Data:** OWNED — LETF registry + 454 AUM anchors + NOII 2020–26 + Alpaca live/historical return. $0 incremental.
- **Universe:** NVDA, TSLA, AMD, GOOGL, MU (+ MSFT/META/AMZN/COIN/PLTR where LETF AUM is material).
- **Expected n & power:** ~hundreds of name-sessions; per-event noise ~20 bps sd → powered only for a mean ≳2–3 bps, so **register as an information-add test (ΔAUC / Δnet-bps vs NOII-only), not a standalone mean-return trade.**
- **A-priori thresholds:** `F_t` must add ≥ +1.0 bps/event net *incremental* over the NOII-only champion on a walk-forward split, OR lift directional hit-rate by ≥2pts with corr < 0.6 to the NOII basis (else it is redundant, not additive).
- **Promotion rule:** forward-paper only (holdout is spent); promote a variant only if the increment survives ≥30 forward sessions.
- **Kill criteria:** `F_t` corr > 0.8 with the observed NOII imbalance (redundant) → KILL; OR incremental net ≤ 0 → KILL and log as EXHAUSTED (mechanism real but non-additive).
- **Trial family charged:** the **auction/champion family** (NOT a new intraday-reversion family — that family is dead per M18).

**Pre-registration KILL recommendation for M19-as-literally-charged:** the "intraday price pressure that mean-reverts" hypothesis should be **killed pre-registration** on primary-mechanics grounds (C1, C4, C7, C10): the re-hedge is a close single-print, not intraday-distributed, so there is no mechanism for intraday pressure to build and revert intraday, and any close→next-session reversal is out-of-mission or already-dead M18.

#### Required closing verdicts
**(a) Timing-split verdict (from PRIMARY language):** **CLOSE-CONCENTRATED.** All four complexes on our names commit in prospectus text to executing the rebalance *at/near the close* against a close-referenced reset (GraniteShares "for execution at close"; Leverage Shares "reset daily, typically at the close of trading"; Direxion/T-Rex "At the close of the markets each trading day, the Adviser rebalances"; Direxion swaps "valued using the closing price"). No issuer discloses a numeric % split, and no issuer discloses routine continuous intraday hedging. Estimated split from primary language: **~default 100% close-window fund-order execution, with an unquantified minority carved off intraday only on high-volatility days.** The swap **counterparty's** own book-hedge fraction (continuous pre-hedge vs. cross) is **UNKNOWN** — but the close-referenced valuation anchors it to the close.

**(b) PIT intraday F-state from disclosures — YES/NO:** **YES.** `F_t = (L²−L)·AUM(t)·r_t` is PIT-computable at the champion's 15:55:10 instant: `(L²−L)` from the owned registry, `AUM(t) ≈ AUM_{T-1}·(1+L·r_t)` from T-1 anchors scaled by the live leveraged return, and `r_t` = live since-prior-close return. The **r-window is disclosure-pinned to since-prior-close** (target = DLF×NAV at end of day; "that day's gains/losses"), *not* a rolling window. **One caveat:** same-day net creation/redemption (shares outstanding) is only disclosed T+1 — approximate with T-1 shares and treat AP flow as bounded noise.

**(c) Does M19 deserve registration? (honest paragraph):** As literally charged — *intraday price pressure that mean-reverts* — **no; it deserves a pre-registration kill.** The primary documents are unanimous that single-stock-LETF re-hedging is a **close single-print event**, not a distributed-intraday one, so there is no mechanical basis for intraday pressure that reverts *intraday*, and the only way to monetize a close→next-session reversal is an out-of-mission overnight hold or the already-falsified M18 gated-intraday-reversion. What is genuinely alive here is the *mechanism*, not the *reversion*: the flow-state proxy `F_t` is real, PIT-computable on owned data, and M12 already showed it explains the champion's name-selectivity. The single defensible registration is therefore a **narrow, low-priority `F_t`-conditioned feature test on the EXISTING close basis trade**, charging the auction family — and it must clear a high novelty bar because the *observed* NOII imbalance the champion already reads at 15:55:10 may render the *modeled* `F_t` redundant (M9's snapshot ceiling). Net recommendation: **kill the intraday-reversion M19; permit at most one `F_t`-as-champion-feature registration, gated on demonstrating incremental info over NOII.**
