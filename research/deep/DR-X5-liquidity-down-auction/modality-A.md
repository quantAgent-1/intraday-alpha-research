## DR-X5 — A academic findings
### Verdict recommendation
**OPEN-TESTABLE** (confidence **MED**) — Peer-reviewed size cross-sections (Bogousslavsky & Muravyev 2010–2018; Goyal–Jegadeesh–Wu 2012–2021) establish that unconditional |auction−4pm mid| rises sharply down the liquidity curve (**~2.66 bps large-cap quintile → ~20.6 bps small-cap quintile**) while end-of-day half-spreads scale in parallel, so **dislocation-per-unit-spread is flat-to-worse for small names** (full-sample |dev|≈ half-spread; large names have a larger pure-impact share). The only size-tier *exception* to “auction is cheaper than continuous” is **Nasdaq microcaps** (Goyal et al. JFQA 2026). No T2 paper re-tabs B&M’s |dev|×size table for 2020–2026; post-2018 evidence is impact models through 2021 plus event/vendor case studies (Russell 2024) — so the **liquidity-down gross prior is load-bearing but DECAY-UNKNOWN**, and the **tradable residual after entry half-spread is the open empirical question**. A ~20-name Nasdaq mid/liquid-small pilot (ADV ~$50–200M, not micro) is the academically justified Stage-A probe: same payer/mechanism as champion, larger raw |dev| prior, NOII cheap, capacity irrelevant; kill if net ≤0 after honest half-spread entry.

What would flip it: (i) owned-data 2020–2026 |near−mid| fire-rate and signed auction-exit means by ADV tier with n≥250 showing **net ≥ +2.5 bps/event after entry half-spread** in mid/small vs champion large; *or* (ii) a clean powered null (net ≤0 / fire-rate collapse / NOII sparse) → EXHAUSTED-BY-FIELD for the smallness thesis as a *net* edge even if gross |dev| is larger.

### Mechanism
**Who pays:** Same price-insensitive **indexed / passive MOC and ETF rebalance flow** documented for the champion (B&M DiD: Auction×Passive / Auction×ETF elasticities strongly positive; active MF ≈0). Auction volume *share* is roughly flat across size quintiles (~5.7–6.1% of ADV), so the payer is present down the curve; absolute auction notional is smaller, but our $1k–$10k size is unconstrained (capacity irrelevant — the thesis).

**Why larger gross dislocation down the curve:** Thinner continuous books + larger relative tick → end-of-day half-spreads dominate the |auction−mid| print (B&M decomposition: full-sample half-spread **7.56 bps** vs pure impact **0.55 bps**; large-cap half-spread **1.47** vs impact **1.19**). Tick Size Pilot (2016) **causally** raised small-stock auction deviations when the tick widened — discrete-tick channel, not “more uninformed flow per se.” Deviations reverse overnight (complete for large; mostly for small) → temporary pressure / bid-ask bounce, not information.

**Why it may *not* net better for us:** Champion edge is filtered |near−mid|≥10 bps *with continuous entry* and auction exit. Entry half-spread at 15:55 scales up as liquidity falls; if |dev| is mostly half-spread, the selectable residual beyond cost may shrink. Goyal et al.: auction price impact **lower than continuous for all but Nasdaq microcaps** — the frontier is **liquid mid/small, not micro**. Sparse auctions (B&M: **9.56%** of small-quintile stock-days have zero auction vs **0.21%** large) and thinner NOII participation are the operational degradations (field inference; schedule parity is T1/modality B).

**Persistence / capacity:** Passive AUM structural; listing-venue close monopoly persists. Our notional is a rounding error vs even small-cap auction ADV fractions. Adversarial risk: informed late flow / toxic closes more material in thin names; higher no-cross rate; odd-lot NBBO understates true retail book for high-priced midcaps.

**Coherent payer found:** yes — indexed MOC into listing-venue close, same family as champion, present across size tiers.

### Economics sketch
- **Expected gross (literature, unconditional |auction−mid|, not champion filter):** Large ~**2.7 bps** | Small ~**20.6 bps** (B&M 2010–18). Full sample ~**8.1 bps**. Reverses overnight (temporary). **Dislocation-per-unit-spread:** full-sample |dev|/half-spread ≈ **8.1/7.6 ≈ 1.07**; large ≈ **2.66/1.47 ≈ 1.8**; small (using EOD full-spread ~60 bps → half ~30) ≈ **20.6/30 ≈ 0.7** — **smallness raises gross, lowers residual-per-spread**.
- **Goyal impact at our size:** even 0.5% ADV is huge vs $10k; our impact **≪1 bp** in non-micro names. Auction fee class **<1 bp** at typical prices.
- **Our cost burden:** Entry continuous half-spread is the load-bearing cost. Priors at 15:55 (order-of-magnitude, continuous market — **not** auction print): liquid large ~**1–3 bps** half; ~$200M ADV mid ~**3–8 bps**; ~$50M ~**8–20 bps**; ~$20M ~**15–40+ bps** (scaled from B&M EOD spreads and market-structure norms; **15:55-specific by ADV = measure on SIP**, academic gap). Zero commission through 2026-12-31.
- **Net prior (champion-style filtered |near−mid|≥10):** If near tracks auction and fire rate rises with noise, **gross selected events may exceed large-cap +2.5**, but **net after 2× entry half-spread often fails to clear 2× cost** in the small quintile. Academic central prior for *net* liquidity-down edge: **0 to +3 bps/event in the $50–200M ADV band; ≤0 below ~$20–30M / micro**. Compare: champion = **+2.5 bps/event dev / +12.5 holdout** (megacap selected).
- **Comparison line:** champion = **+2.5 bps/event dev / +12.5 holdout**.
