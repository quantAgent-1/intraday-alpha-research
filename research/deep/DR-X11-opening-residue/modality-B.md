## DR-X11 — Nasdaq opening-cross imbalance residue: venue mechanics (modality B, primary/venue)

### Verdict recommendation
**OPEN-TESTABLE (confidence MED)** — but only as an *informational*-continuation bet, NOT a mechanical-payer one. The decisive mechanics question is now RESOLVED against F3's strongest rationale: unpaired on-open QUANTITY (the NOII `imbalance_shares`) is composed of MOO/LOO/OIO orders that "may be executed **only** in the Nasdaq Opening Cross"; any unexecuted portion is **CANCELLED at the 9:30 cross, not converted to the continuous book** (Rule 4702(b)(8)–(10); FAQ Q18). So "the residue completes in continuous trading after 09:30" is **merely informational — there is no mechanical order-flow payer**. The mechanics do not *forbid* a test on owned data (data owned; 9:28:30 signal exists in every regime; entry 09:35 is manual-latency-compatible), but they remove the price-insensitive-payer property that makes the champion score 5/5. Survivor-profile score ≈ **2/5**.

**What would flip it:** finding, in a rule/spec, that unexecuted MOO/LOO/OIO *reprice-and-rest* on the continuous book after the cross (they do not — verified in exact rule text). Absent that, only the DATA modality can flip the lane: if `imbalance_shares` at 9:28:30 carries incremental continuation signal that survives cost and top-session exclusion — otherwise the lane folds into the already-closed M6b/M24.

### Mechanism
**Who pays / why price-insensitive:** In the *closing* champion, indexed MOC/rebalance flow *must* print at the cross → a price-insensitive payer you trade WITH into a single print. F3 asks whether the analogous *opening* imbalance creates pressure that **persists past the 9:30 print** into continuous trading. Venue mechanics say it does not:

- The NOII "Imbalance" is defined (Rule 4752(a)(2)) as "the number of shares of buy or sell **MOO, LOO or Early Market Hours** orders that may not be matched with other MOO, LOO, Early Market Hours, or OIO order shares." These dedicated on-open orders "may be executed **only** in the Nasdaq Opening Cross" (Rule 4702(b)(8)–(10)). Unexecuted → **cancellation message returned after the cross** (FAQ Q18). The shares *vanish* at 9:30; they exert zero continuation pressure.
- The **only** interest that carries into continuous trading is (a) "Open Eligible Interest" (SDAY/SGTC/SHEX/GTMC continuous-book orders) and (b) "Opening Cross/Market Hours Orders" (a regular DAY/GTC order *flagged* to the open, which "operat[es] thereafter in accordance with its designated Order Type … if not executed in full"). But these are **limit** orders that did NOT clear at the cross price → they rest **passively** on the far side of the cross. They are resting liquidity, not directional pressure — the *opposite* of a continuation payer.
- **By order type:** MOO (market, cross-only) → cancel; LOO/TIF-On-Open (limit, cross-only) → cancel; OIO (imbalance-only, executes only against MOO/LOO/Early, never adds to imbalance) → cancel; Early-Market-Hours MDAY/MGTC & Opening-Cross/Market-Hours orders → **rest passively** per own TIF (MIOC → cancel).

**Why it persists / capacity:** N/A — no mechanical edge exists to persist. Any residual predictability is *informational* (the imbalance reveals latent institutional direction that the same actors may re-express after 9:30). That channel is weak and crowded: real-time NOII is cheap and public (below), so the information is fully priced by open. **Coherent price-insensitive payer for QUANTITY-continuation: NOT FOUND.**

### Claims
C1 [CONFIRMED] (T1, rule text operative 2021-05-17, panel 2020→2026): Unexecuted **MOO, LOO, and OIO** orders are **CANCELLED** at the Opening Cross, never posted to the continuous book — each "may be executed only in the Nasdaq Opening Cross"; "a cancellation message will be returned to the firm after the cross occurs." — Nasdaq Rule 4702(b)(8)–(10) [S1]; Crosses FAQ Q18 [S2].
C2 [CONFIRMED] (T1, effective **May 17, 2021**): SR-NASDAQ-2021-004 added the **Early Order Imbalance Indicator (EOII)** — "Beginning at 9:25 a.m., Nasdaq shall disseminate … an early Order Imbalance Indicator **every 10 seconds** until the Order Imbalance Indicator begins to disseminate," containing "the same information as the Order Imbalance Indicator, except that it will **exclude information about indicative prices**" (no Near/Far). Full OII continues "**every second**" from 9:28 to open. Rulebook redline banner: "changes … will not be implemented until May 17, 2021." — Rule 4752(a)(1),(d)(1) [S1]; Equity Trader Alert #2021-29 [S6]; SEC order 34-91480 / FR 2021-07197 [S5].
C3 [CONFIRMED] (T1): NOII field semantics — **Current Reference Price** = single price at/within Nasdaq BBO maximizing paired MOO/LOO/OIO/Early shares; **Imbalance** and **Paired** shares are measured **at the Current Reference Price**; **Near Clearing Price** = price at which cross orders **AND Open Eligible (continuous-book) Interest** would execute; **Far Clearing Price** = price at which **only** the opening-book (cross) orders would execute. — Rule 4752(a)(3)(A)–(E) [S1]; Fact Sheet NOII diagram [S3].
C4 [CONFIRMED] (T1, current): Opening cutoffs — MOO must be received **prior to 9:28** (after → rejected); LOO acceptable until **9:29:30** (late LOO 9:28–9:29:30 accepted at limit, else repriced to the more aggressive of First/Second Opening Reference Price; IOC-LOO after 9:29:30 rejected); OIO enterable until the cross; **cancel/modify locked at 9:25** for all on-open + Market-Hours orders. — Rule 4702(b)(8)–(10), 4752(a)(10) [S1]; FAQ Q17, Q25 [S2].
C5 [CONFIRMED] (T1): Opening-cross price is collared — cross price must fall within a **benchmark ± threshold** band (retail equities threshold = **greater of $0.50 or 10%** of QBBO midpoint; ETPs 5%/$0.50) and pass one of **Opening Cross Price Tests A/B/C** (A: prev-day NOCP ± thr; B: Nasdaq last sale after 9:15 ± thr; C: Nasdaq best bid/offer ± thr). **Fail → all MOO/LOO/OIO/Early orders cancelled, no cross, security opens in continuous trading.** — Rule 4752(d)(2)(E),(F) [S1]; FAQ Q3–Q4 [S2].
C6 [CONFIRMED] (T1+T3): The owned lake maps 1:1 to spec — `ref_price`=Current Reference Price, `near_price`=Near Clearing Price (Databento `cont_book_clr_price`, cross+continuous), `far_price`=Far Clearing Price (Databento `auct_interest_clr_price`, cross-only), `imbalance_shares`=size of Imbalance at ref, `paired_shares`=paired at ref, `side`=B/S/N/O. ITCH NOII message type **'I'** carries a **Cross Type** field (O=Opening, C=Closing, H=Halt/IPO, A=Extended-close) = Databento `auction_type` — this is how open vs close is separated in the lake. — TotalView-ITCH 5.0 spec [S4]; Databento imbalance schema [S7]; Rule 4752(a)(3) [S1].
C7 [CONFIRMED] (T1/T2): LULD does **not** collar the opening cross — LULD price bands operate only **09:30–16:00** (i.e., *after* the cross concludes), and the 09:30–09:35 LULD reference price is *set by* the opening-auction price. The opening cross is bounded by its own Price Tests A/B/C (C5), not by LULD. — LULD Plan / Nasdaq LULD FAQ [S9]; Rule 4752(d) [S1].
C8 [CONFIRMED] (T3, Nasdaq marketing/filing rationale, 2021): Nasdaq's own stated use-case for opening imbalance data is **cross-participation decision support** — it "gives auction participants a look at the supply and demand … prior to the opening and closing crosses" so they "place orders more intelligently" (i.e., to *offset* imbalances in the cross). No claim of a post-open continuation edge. EOII deliberately **omits** Near/Far "to enhance stability … reducing the possibility of large indicative price movements during the early moments of the price formation process." — Nasdaq auction-process article [S10]; FR 2021-07197 rationale [S5].
C9 [PLAUSIBLE] (T1/T3): Real-time NOII is delivered inside **Nasdaq TotalView-ITCH**, which is broadly and cheaply accessible (retail-broker resale ~$1–5/mo; non-professional depth ~$9–20/mo; professional depth ~$76–180/mo direct). Wide access ⇒ the opening-imbalance signal is **crowded** and informationally efficient by 9:30. — TotalView product page + venue fee comparisons [S4],[S7]; project note (real-time NOII ≈ $199/mo bundle, not purchased).
C10 [CONFIRMED] (T1, effective 2025): SR-NASDAQ-2024-065 (SEC 34-102336, approved 2025-02-04) added **Halt Cross Price Protections** and a **Hybrid Closing Cross** — halt/close-focused; no material change found to the *regular opening cross* imbalance dissemination or fields. Flag as a minor adjacent structural date. — FR 2025-02386 / 34-102336 [S8].

### Economics sketch
- **Champion benchmark:** +2.5 bps/event dev; **+12.5 bps/event holdout** — single event, single-print exit, no exit spread.
- **F3 cost burden:** two continuous taker crossings. Megacap RT taker floor ≈ **2–5 bps/leg → 4–10 bps round-trip**; semis (MU/AMD-class) up to **~16 bps/leg → ~32 bps RT** — plus continuous-market fill ambiguity on *both* legs (the exact trap that inflated the program's phantom +152 bps winner).
- **Net prior:** to clear the §3 "≥2× cost" gate, F3 needs gross continuation ≥ **~8–20 bps** (megacaps) over 09:35→≤11:00, sign-aligned with the 9:28:30 imbalance. There is **no venue/mechanical source** for such pressure (flow cancels), and the two nearest pre-registered tests already died: **M6b** (naive opening-cross imbalance) FAILED; **M24** (near-vs-ref price dislocation fade at 09:28:30) closed BETWEEN THE BARS. Expected net ≈ **≤0 after cost**, before top-session concentration risk.

### Proposed next test (informational reframing only — mechanics do not supply a payer)
- **Hypothesis:** normalized `imbalance_shares` at **09:28:30** (e.g. signed imbalance / (paired+imbalance), or / ADV) predicts the SIGN of the continuous return **09:35 → exit ≤11:00**, as a purely *informational* signal.
- **Named payer:** **NONE mechanical** — state explicitly that unexecuted on-open flow cancels; the only claim is that dedicated on-open imbalance *reveals* latent institutional direction re-expressed after 9:30. This is a ≤2/5 candidate and must be registered as such.
- **Data:** OWNED, $0 — NOII open-window (`imbalance_shares`,`paired_shares`,`side`,`ref_price`,`near_price`,`far_price`) + 1 s XNAS top-of-book (10 names) for the fill proxy.
- **Universe:** the **28-name broad set (2023→2026)** is entirely post-2021-05-17 → stationary; core-5 usable back to 2020 **only if sampled at ≥9:28** (near/far/imbalance all live by 9:28 in every regime — see stationarity note). Sample **exactly at 09:28:30** to be regime-invariant.
- **Expected n & power:** thousands of name-days; statistically a ~1–2 bps mean is detectable, but the **economic** hurdle (~8–20 bps net) sits far above the statistical one — power is not the binding constraint, the cost floor is.
- **A-priori thresholds:** pre-register a directional hit-rate AND a **net-of-double-taker-cost** mean ≥ 2× the continuous floor (≥ ~8 bps megacap), evaluated **ex-top-5-sessions** (AMD/MU concentration trap).
- **Promotion rule:** survives cost + top-session exclusion on the stationary 28-name set AND adds incremental info beyond the M24 near-vs-ref dislocation feature.
- **Kill criteria:** net ex-top-5 < cost floor, OR the quantity signal is subsumed by the already-killed M24 price-dislocation feature → return **EXHAUSTED-BY-US** (folds into M6b/M24).
- **Trial family charged:** opening-cross-imbalance family (M6b/M24 lineage), **quantity-axis sub-branch = F3**.
